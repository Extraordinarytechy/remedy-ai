RemedyAI checks a broken product against official sources: the maker's warranty and free repair programs, card warranty benefits and consumer law. It tells you what to do next and prepares a claim. If no source covers the case, it says so.

What it covers today

  • Apple One (1) Year Limited Warranty (U.S.): iPhone (including iPhone 18 Pro), iPad, iPod, Apple TV, HomePod and Apple Vision Pro, faults in the first year.
  • Apple's U.S. one-year warranties for Mac, Apple Watch and AirPods, AirTag and Apple accessories: faults in the first year.
  • Mac mini (2023, M2) no-power program and iPhone 14 Plus rear camera program: free repair for 3 years from first sale; serial check required.
  • Google Consumer Hardware Limited Warranty (U.S. and Canada): Pixel phones, tablets, watches and earbuds, faults in the first year. Pixel 9 Pro and 9 Pro XL Extended Repair Program: vertical display line, free display repair for 3 years from purchase after an inspection.
  • Samsung 12-month built-in limited warranty (U.S.): Galaxy phones, including Fold and Flip, faults in the first year.
  • Visa Infinite Extended Warranty Protection (U.S.): any brand, one extra year on warranties of 3 years or less.
  • UK consumer rights on faulty goods: any brand bought from a UK store, up to 6 years to claim (5 in Scotland).

Every source is re-checked daily; live status at /api/sources. Examples: /api/fixtures.
