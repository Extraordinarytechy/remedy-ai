RemedyAI: your warranty ended. That does not always mean you pay.

Manufacturers run free repair programs for known faults, some credit cards extend warranties, and consumer law can outlast both. RemedyAI checks a case against the published source for each route, shows why it matched and what could stop it, and prepares a claim PDF. If no source covers the case, it says so.

Routes it checks (each tied to a primary source)

  • Apple iPhone 14 Plus Service Program for Rear Camera Issue: free repair for 3 years from first retail sale; serial check required.
  • Apple iPhone 12 / 12 Pro no-sound program: page still online but no longer on Apple's service-program index.
  • Visa Infinite Extended Warranty Protection: +1 year on eligible warranties of 3 years or less.
  • UK consumer rights on faulty goods: up to 6 years to claim (5 in Scotland).

Source Watch

Every source above is re-fetched daily. A route whose source page disappears, or whose Apple program leaves Apple's index, is downgraded to "re-verify first". Live status: /api/sources.
